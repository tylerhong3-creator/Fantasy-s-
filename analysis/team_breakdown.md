### Priorities

1. **PTS (playoffs).** The biggest gap against playoff teams. Every trade above adds points.
2. **AST.** HBB ranks you 9th on projected top-10 players; one assist-heavy scorer moves it most per unit of talent.
3. **BLK and FG%.** Swing categories. Your bigs (Mobley, Clingan, KAT, Jabari) carry both, so keep them.
