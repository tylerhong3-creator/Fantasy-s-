## 2. The first-round decision

**Consensus top 3 (every major 9-cat board):** Nikola Jokić (DEN), Victor Wembanyama (SAS), Shai Gilgeous-Alexander (OKC).

**At #4, your priority list:**
1. If one of Jokić, Wembanyama or SGA is still there (casual ESPN leagues sometimes take Luka or Giannis in the top 3), **take him.**
2. Otherwise, take **Luka Dončić (LAL)**. ESPN's category rankings have him 4th, and ESPN's own experts took him at #4 in their 10-team category mock. RotoWire, RotoBaller and Yahoo also have him 4th.
   - 2025-26: about 33.5 pts, 7.7 reb, 8.3 ast, 1.6 stl and 4.0 3PM in 64 games.
   - LeBron is gone (to PHI), so the Lakers offense is entirely his. Walker Kessler gives him a lob target.
   - The costs: about 4 turnovers a game, and roughly 78% FT on heavy volume. You'll usually concede TO. That's fine, because he wins PTS, 3PM, AST and STL and helps REB.
3. **Alternative if you want less volatility:** Tyrese Maxey (PHI). In 2025-26 he averaged 28.3 pts, 6.6 ast, 1.9 stl, 0.8 blk and 3.1 3PM, with good FT% and low TO. That is an elite 9-cat profile. The risk is a crowded Philadelphia roster (LeBron, Jaylen Brown, Embiid), which should cut his usage.

**Other first-round names, picks 5-10 in ESPN's expert mock:** Anthony Edwards, Jalen Johnson, Cade Cunningham, Jayson Tatum, Tyrese Maxey, Donovan Mitchell. If Luka goes before #4 and you don't want Maxey, take **Edwards**. He offers scoring, 3s, steals and good FG% with low assists.

## 3. Recommended build: "Luka + efficiency + blocks"

Luka gives you PTS, 3PM, AST, STL and some REB. Build around him like this:
- **Concede TO.** Don't spend picks fixing turnovers.
- **Protect FT% and FG%** with efficient guards and wings, and with bigs who don't take many free throws. A bad FT shooter on high volume, like Giannis, hurts FT% far more than a bad shooter on low volume.
- **Get 2-3 real shot-blockers by about round 7.** Blocks are the scarcest category. Point guards are the deepest position.
- **Don't punt in rounds 1-3.** After pick 44 or 57, look at your roster, find your weakest 1-2 categories, and stop chasing them. In H2H you only need to win 5 of 9 each week.

**The ESPN experts' blueprint from this exact slot (10-team category mock):**
Luka (4) → Derrick White (17) → James Harden (24) → Mikal Bridges (37) → Chet Holmgren (44) → Rudy Gobert (57) → LeBron James (64).

## 4. Round-by-round targets

Each list is in rough priority order. Take the best player left who fits your build.

### Pick 17 (round 2)
- **Tyrese Haliburton (IND).** He missed 2025-26 with the Achilles tear and is fully cleared. His assists, 3s, steals, FT% and low TO are a perfect match for Luka. Yahoo ADP is about 13-15, so he may already be gone.
- **Kawhi Leonard (TOR).** He was traded to Toronto. In 2025-26 he averaged 27.9 pts, 1.9 stl and 2.6 3PM at about 50% FG and high FT% over 65 games. Expect load management.
- **Derrick White (BOS).** 3s, steals, blocks, FT% and low TO. His role grows with Jaylen Brown gone.
- **Kevin Durant (HOU).** FG%, FT%, 3s and blocks. He turns 38 this season.
- Jamal Murray (DEN), Donovan Mitchell (CLE), Scottie Barnes (TOR), Karl-Anthony Towns (NYK).

### Pick 24 (round 3)
Whoever is left from the pick-17 list, plus:
- **James Harden (CLE)**: assists, 3s, FT% and steals. He's high-TO, but you're already conceding that.
- **Austin Reaves (LAL)**: ESPN ADP is about 39, much later than Yahoo's 23, so he may last into round 4.
- **Alperen Şengün (HOU), Evan Mobley (CLE), Jaren Jackson Jr., Jalen Williams (OKC).**
- **Amen Thompson (HOU)**, but only if you decide to punt FT% and 3PM.

### Picks 37 and 44 (rounds 4-5): ESPN discount zone
- **Chet Holmgren (OKC).** ESPN ADP is about 64, versus 28 on Yahoo. In 2025-26 he averaged about 1.9 blocks with good FG%, FT% and some 3s. Target him at 37 or 44, not earlier.
- **Trey Murphy III (NOP).** ESPN ADP is about 75, versus 40 on Yahoo. He averaged 21.5 pts, 5.7 reb, 3.8 ast and 1.5 stl in 2025-26 and is healthy now. You can get him at 44 or even 57.
- **Mikal Bridges (NYK), Jalen Williams (OKC), Dyson Daniels (ATL, steals monster), Darius Garland (LAC).**
- **Anthony Davis (WAS)** if you want upside. He played only 20 games last season, but his first-round per-game value is priced in by round 5.

### Picks 57 and 64 (rounds 6-7)
- **Walker Kessler (LAL).** ESPN ADP is about 79, versus 39 on Yahoo. He's the Lakers' starting center, with elite blocks, rebounds and FG%. The risk is the shoulder, since he played only 5 games last season.
- **Rudy Gobert (MIN), Jarrett Allen (CLE), Myles Turner (MIL)**: blocks and FG%.
- **LeBron James (PHI)**: still top-50 value in round 7.
- **Guards:** Fred VanVleet (HOU, back from his ACL), Immanuel Quickley (TOR), Jalen Suggs (ORL), Josh Giddey (CHI), Josh Hart (NYK), Stephon Castle (SAS).
- **Cameron Boozer (MEM)**, the first rookie off the board.

### Picks 77-97 (rounds 8-10)
- **Bigs:** Kel'el Ware (MIL, ESPN ADP ~102), Zach Edey (MEM, ~122), Day'Ron Sharpe (BKN starting C, ~139), Donovan Clingan (POR), Alex Sarr (WAS), Onyeka Okongwu (ATL).
- **Guards and wings:** Ajay Mitchell (OKC), Cason Wallace (OKC), Herb Jones (NOP), Jaden McDaniels (MIN), Ty Jerome (MEM), Cedric Coward (MEM), Naz Reid (CHA), Dejounte Murray (NOP).
- **Rookies:** AJ Dybantsa (WAS, ankle, may miss the opener; ESPN ADP ~76), Caleb Wilson (CHI, ~75).

### Picks 104-124 (rounds 11-13): upside and stashes
- **IR stashes:** Jimmy Butler (GSW, out until at least about Jan 2), Brandon Ingram (LAC, Achilles, out to start the season).
- **Upside:** Collin Murray-Boyles (TOR), Matas Buzelis (CHI), Khaman Maluach (PHX; Mark Williams is hurt), Darryn Peterson (UTA), Kevin Porter Jr. (MIL), Payton Pritchard (BOS).
- **Draft for streaming:** your last 2-3 picks are the easiest players to cut. In ESPN H2H with daily lineups, games played per week matter, so stream the waiver wire.
